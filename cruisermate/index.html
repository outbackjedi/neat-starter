The display

Five gauges. Your five.

The bar display fits the space above the centre stack. It uses an anti-glare panel and matching matte trim, and dims with your headlights. Pick any readings below to see how your layout would look.

Choose up to 5 readings · 5 selected

Daylight readable

Optically bonded, 1000-nit IPS panel with an anti-glare coating. It stays readable in direct outback sun and drops to a soft glow at night.

Gloves-on touch

Swipe between pages and tap to acknowledge an alert. Large hit targets work on corrugations and with gloves on.

Alerts that matter

Set your own limits for EGT, coolant, trans temp or voltage. The bar flashes amber and the app sends a notification.

The app

Full control from your phone.

Use the CruiserMate app to change layouts, colours, units and alert limits, update firmware, and review every trip. Changes reach the display in about a second over Bluetooth.

  • Layout builder. Drag gauges into place and choose between numbers, bars or sparklines.
  • Themes. Factory white, amber or night red, with brightness curves you can adjust.
  • Trip logs. Every drive is recorded at 20 Hz and stored on the unit. Export to CSV or sync to the cloud.
  • Diagnostics. Read and clear fault codes, check DPF soot load, and trigger a regen where supported.
  • Profiles. Save Highway, Towing and Low-range layouts and switch with one tap.

Install

Installs in about 30 minutes. No cutting.

  1. 01

    Plug in

    Connect the logger module to the OBD2 port under the dash. The loom runs behind the existing trim.

  2. 02

    Mount

    A model-specific bracket clips the bar display into the dash. Nothing is drilled and it removes cleanly.

  3. 03

    Pair

    Open the app, tap the display to pair, and pick a starting layout. You're ready to drive.

FAQ

Questions

Will it drain my battery?

No. The unit sleeps when the ignition is off and draws less than 1 mA, so it's fine for long stays in camp.

Does it affect my warranty?

CruiserMate only reads the OBD2 port and doesn't change the ECU. Nothing is cut or spliced, and it removes in minutes.

Does it need phone signal?

No. The display and logging work fully offline. The app connects over Bluetooth, and cloud sync waits until you're back in range.

Can I add my own sensors?

Yes. The auxiliary port accepts the optional EGT probe and two analogue inputs, for example for a rear diff or auxiliary battery.

Is it affiliated with Toyota?

No. CruiserMate is an independent aftermarket product designed to match the factory interior.
